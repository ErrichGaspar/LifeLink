LifeLink is a Blood Donation Management System designed to make the management of blood donors and donation records more organized and efficient. It provides a centralized platform for managing donor information, checking donor eligibility, and tracking donation status, helping healthcare organizations handle blood donation activities more effectively.

About LifeLink

LifeLink was developed with the goal of supporting a more efficient and reliable blood donation process. It focuses on helping healthcare organizations maintain accurate donor information while making donor management easier and more systematic.

More than just a record-keeping system, LifeLink aims to encourage proper donor monitoring and promote the importance of blood donation. Through technology, the system seeks to contribute to better coordination between donors and organizations involved in blood donation.
