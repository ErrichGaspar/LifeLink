LifeLink is a student-built blood donation management system designed to make donation and blood-request processes more organized, accessible, and convenient.

Explore LifeLink

What is LifeLink?

LifeLink helps donors track donation schedules, eligibility, and history while giving administrators tools to manage donor records, appointments, blood inventory, and blood requests in one centralized system. Our goal is simple: make the process easier for the people who give blood and the people who need it.

The Problem We Saw

Blood donation information is often handled through manual records and coordination. This can make donor eligibility, donation history, appointments, inventory, and requests harder to monitor and more prone to inconsistent information.

Less unnecessary processing. More organized information. Easier monitoring. A more convenient experience.

Why We Built LifeLink

LifeLink began as a Software Design course requirement. As future Computer Engineers, we also wanted to explore how technology can respond to real needs in communities—especially in healthcare. This is our small attempt to turn that idea into something practical.

What LifeLink Does

Donor Management

Organizes donor information in one place for accurate monitoring.

Eligibility & Readiness

Supports health assessment and donor eligibility review before donation.

Donation Scheduling

Organizes appointments digitally and reduces unnecessary coordination.

Donation History

Keeps an accessible record of completed donations.

Blood Inventory

Helps administrators monitor blood stock by blood type.

Blood Requests

Provides a more organized way to submit and manage requests.

How LifeLink Helps

For Donors

Register → Check Eligibility → View History → Schedule Donation → Donate

For Administrators

Donor Records → Eligibility → Donations → Appointments → Inventory → Requests

For Requesters

Submit Request → Information Recorded → Administrator Reviews → Request Managed

Our Vision

We envision technology helping make healthcare processes more organized and accessible. LifeLink does not claim to solve every problem, but we believe even small improvements can be a step toward something better in the Philippines.

Our Academic Journey

LifeLink was developed during the first semester of third year in the BS Computer Engineering program at Bulacan State University – Bustos Campus, as part of the Software Design course. It allowed us to apply software development, design, quality assurance, and consultation to a real-world problem.

Academic Project Disclaimer

LifeLink is currently an academic project and prototype developed for educational purposes. It is not a hospital, blood bank, medical institution, or replacement for professional healthcare services. Information in the system is not medical advice.
